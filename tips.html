TRAVEL SMARTER

Simple Travel Tips

Small preparations can make a big difference.

01

Plan before you go

Check the weather, travel dates, transport options and opening hours of the places you want to visit.

02

Pack light

Make a list of essentials and pack clothes suitable for the weather. Keep important items easy to reach.

03

Keep documents safe

Carry the required identity documents and keep digital copies in a secure place.

04

Respect local culture

Learn a few local words, follow local rules and be respectful towards people and nature.

05

Keep some flexibility

Leave a little free time in your schedule. Sometimes the best travel moments are unplanned.
